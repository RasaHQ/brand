# Logos reference

Logo Full (the RASA speech bubble) by default; the icon (R) where space is tight or the brand is already established. Taupe Dark #181210 on light grounds, Taupe Light #FAF6F0 on dark grounds, Rasa Pink #E85A91 for accent moments only. On slides: 32px high at x 80, y 48; 64px and centred on Opening and Closing. No recolouring outside these three inks, no stretching, rotation, outlines, shadows or effects; keep the clear space. GitHub may hide a Taupe Light logo on a light page: choose by the documented ink, not the thumbnail.
