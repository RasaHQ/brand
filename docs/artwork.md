# Artwork direction

Brand artwork follows the Rasa Brand 2026 [visual layer](../design-system/rasa-brand-2026/README.md#3-visual-layer) and, for rasa.community covers, the [community card system](community-cards.md). This page covers artwork that is neither a slide nor a cover card: hero images, social graphics, explainer stills.

## Vocabulary

- **Grounds:** Taupe Light `#FAF6F0` or Taupe Dark `#181210`. Taupe 50 `#FFFBF5` is the brightest surface (cards, windows). No white, no greys, no gradients.
- **Accent:** Rasa Pink `#E85A91` marks the single focus: a highlighted node, row, path or turn. Small pink text is Pink 600 `#AA1E54` on light grounds.
- **Categories:** secondary hues in deck order (periwinkle, seafoam, gold, coral, orchid, lavender, sage), one colour per meaning; tints at 100, shapes at 300, text at 700; gold shifts darker.
- **Texture:** one Chladni or Soundwave pattern per layout, bleeding off at least one edge, never behind text; accent treatment for hero moments only.
- **Type:** STK Bureau Serif headline, Sans body, Mono eyebrow; Google substitutes where STK is not installed.

## Composition recipes

### Product moment

One product window (`ProductWindow`) or `StudioShell` with real copy only where the image points; `Skeleton` everywhere else; no browser chrome. One focus element. At most six rows.

### How it works

Concept cards (`ConceptCard`, `Node`, `Hub`, `Flow`) joined by orthogonal connectors: straight runs, 12px rounded corners, 1.5px Taupe 700, Pink 400 for the one focus path, one arrowhead at the end the flow goes to. Never curved or freeform. Don't mix concept cards and product windows unless a concept diagram zooms into one real window.

### Evidence

`NumeralStat` for claims about Rasa itself ("8 years …"), `ProofCard` for named customers (sector, name, one figure). The claim always states the number. Verify every number, customer and condition before publishing.

### Editorial title or event graphic

A short serif statement, a Mono eyebrow in `fg-muted`, optional one-line Sans subtitle, Logo Full, and either one texture or one purposeful diagram. Generous negative space. For rasa.community covers use the community card system instead of composing freely.

## Production defaults (adapted)

| Output | Canvas | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slide | 1920 × 1080 | Use the slide layer: 12-column grid, safe area x 80–1840, y 48–1032 |
| rasa.community cover / OG | 1200 × 630 | Community card system only |
| Showcase build header | 1200 × 400 | Community card system |
| Social square | 1080 × 1080 | Recompose; don't crop a landscape diagram |
| Portrait | 1080 × 1920 | Recompose vertically; keep platform overlay zones clear |
| Video | 1920 × 1080 | Frame-driven; see [motion.md](motion.md) |

Prefer SVG for diagrams and icons, PNG for rasterised UI, WebP for web backgrounds. Check text floors at final size: 24px for slide-level text, 14px inside visuals.

## Image generation

Use generated imagery only for supporting, non-UI illustration. Never ask a model for the logo, interface text, diagrams or numbers; build those from components and composite approved logos after.

> Create an editorial illustration about [specific subject] for Rasa. Warm Taupe Light background (#FAF6F0), Taupe Dark structure (#181210), Taupe 200–300 supporting shapes (#F3EEE7, #C8C0B6) and one small Rasa Pink (#E85A91) focal detail. Compose [objects and relationships] with generous negative space, flat fills and clean geometry. Leave [area] clear for typography added later. [Ratio and size]. No text, lettering, logos, gradients, glass, neon, purple, robots, brains or circuit boards; no invented product screens or claims.

## Review checklist

- Can a viewer say what the image shows in one sentence?
- Exactly one pink focus; every other colour carries a meaning?
- Connectors orthogonal; no left-edge accent stripes; no dark chat bubbles on light grounds?
- Text at or above the floors, inside the safe area, no orphans or split product names?
- Real fonts, or the Google substitutes stated?
- Claims, customers and third-party marks approved?

`artwork/diagram-starter.svg` is an editable starter that follows these rules (focus path, orthogonal connectors, Taupe cards). `artwork/brand-reference.svg` is a token sheet. Both are generated by `npm --prefix design-system run reference`.
